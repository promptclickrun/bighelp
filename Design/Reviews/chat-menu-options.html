The Bot Chat fix

For the conversation mismatch, make both the home Chat entry and Agents → Message resolve the selected profile’s canonical Bot Chat, like Hermes Desktop. Keep explicit New chat and saved-history entries unchanged.

If the canonical lookup fails, keep the current screen and offer a retry rather than silently opening an unrelated conversation. Ignore a lookup that finishes after you switch agents, computers or screens. Preserve drafts and both conversations.
