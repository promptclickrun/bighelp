bighelp public avatar catalog

Faces and Shapes are exported from the app renderers.
Halloween and official bighelp mascot artwork were supplied by the bighelp maintainer for publication.

blobatar
Upstream: https://github.com/Alain00/blobatar (npm package "blobatar" 2.0.0)
Used for: Agent Studio's Face avatars (Bighelp/Companion/HermesFaces/HermesBlobFace.swift
is a Swift port of its seed hashing, traits, palette and shapes, so faces match
Hermes Desktop).
